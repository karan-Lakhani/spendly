Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type and server logs, used to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to create and secure your account, store and display your expenses, and generate the summaries and filters you see in the app. We do not sell your personal data or use your expenses for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database managed by Spendly. Passwords are stored as hashes, never in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may send a request, including your IP address, to Google's servers. We do not share your account or expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
